F5 Long Block · Campaign plan · Madsen × FPT

Starting point

A product nobody searches for yet

The product

F5 Long Block

A new FPT long block for the F5 engines in Case IH, New Holland and CASE machines. Sold in Canada through Madsen Power Systems.

The goal

Quote requests

From mechanics, repair shops and dealers, in agriculture and construction, in Ontario and Quebec, in English and French.

The question

Create demand

People search by machine model and part number, not for "F5 Long Block". How do we reach them before the engine fails?

Strategy · who buys

Who buys

Primary

The mechanic

Independent shops and dealer service bays.

Pain: a tractor stuck in the shop in season, the customer calling every day.

Searches: model and engine code.

Convinced by: exact fit, in stock, original parts reused.

Google · Meta

End user

The owner

Farmers, rental fleets, contractors.

Pain: a machine down puts a season or a contract at risk.

Searches: brand, model, "engine replacement".

Convinced by: keeping the machine they trust, back to work fast.

Meta

Channel

The parts manager

Case IH and New Holland dealers, parts resellers.

Pain: too many SKUs, slow stock.

Searches: part numbers, distributor terms.

Convinced by: one block covers many applications, with Madsen stock behind it.
